# Third-Party Notices

IrisAura uses the third-party components listed below. Each remains under its own licence.
The exact resolved versions, including transitive packages, are listed in
[docs/DEPENDENCIES.md](docs/DEPENDENCIES.md) (generated by `scripts/generate-dependency-inventory.ps1`).

Only components that are **redistributed** in a shipped product need their licence text bundled in
the installer and in *Settings → About → Third-party notices* (BLUEPRINT §13). Build-time and
test-only components are listed for completeness.
